About

Stephen Hollingshead, Ph.D.

Managing Director

ChangeInEx CEO Dr. Stephen Hollingshead helps create jobs in tough spots using technology to improve and extend property rights governance.

A former financial regulator and cabinet-level economic advisor, he founded ChangeInEx to address the global de-risking crisis (banks fleeing developing markets because of post-9/11 regulation) and created the Secured Special Economic Zone (SSEZ) model for investment in post-conflict environments.

Both these tools emerged from his work to combat genocide and the refugee crisis, and they share a common theme: The only enduring solution is to give families the dignity of productive work. That means giving basic property rights (identity, title, and exchange) to workers most in need, and improving property rights for investors and entrepreneurs where investment is most needed.

Background

Stephen Hollingshead grew up in an entrepreneurial family; his father ran companies in Michigan, California, Guam, Minnesota, and Texas. At 16, he dropped out of high school. But 10 years later, he had a Ph.D., had taught government at the university level, and had become the youngest-ever winner of a primary election for US Congress.

Another 10 years later, he had co-founded one school and turned another into a National Blue Ribbon School, written a book on Shakespeare’s politics, been a marketing executive, started two companies, and served as executive director of a national tax reform group.

Dr. Hollingshead then served in the Bush Administration as Senior Advisor to the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development, and as Deputy Assistant Secretary for Enforcement. The White House and OPM recognized his management with a $25,000 Award for Extraordinary Accomplishment, rare for a political appointee. While he coordinated the President’s Management Agenda for HUD, the GAO removed HUD from its High Risk List for the first time. As the $40 billion department’s chief enforcement official, he realigned its six enforcement centers, eliminating the long-standing backlog of 4,500 aged cases while increasing portfolio recoveries.

His passion to promote peace through free enterprise led him to found the Friends of Libya Foundation and IraqHaven.org. He has traveled widely in the Middle East, Africa, and Latin America.

As a board director, he helped guide MERSCORP Holdings, the mortgage industry utility that operates the Mortgage Electronic Registration System, through a period of regulatory and legal risk to a successful sale to Intercontinental Exchange (NYSE: ICE). He is Lead Director of LevelField Financial, in its acquisition of the soon-to-be LevelField Bank, and serves on the advisory board of the College of Science and Engineering at Houston Christian University.

Dr. Hollingshead is founder and CEO of regtech startup ChangeInEx, Inc., chairman of the Center for American Interests and Policy, and principal owner of the economic and business development consulting companies Candor LLC and Brillantegia S de RL de CV. He regularly briefs executive and legislative branch officials on policy improvements. He is a member of the Philadelphia Society and the Council to Modernize Governance, and a fellow of the German Marshall Fund of the US and the Club for Growth Foundation.
