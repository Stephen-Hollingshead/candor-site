01

Zones with Regulatory Framework Agreements

Zones expand on the successful Opportunity Zones from the 2017 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, designating federal lands (and potentially private or state-owned lands via state Mirror Zone legislation) for ultra-low taxes and streamlined regulations to attract investment.

Because regulation costs more than taxes, the regulatory streamlining is worth more than the tax breaks, especially as it shortens the time before economic activity can begin. The Regulatory Framework Agreement (RFA) in each zone is the growth opportunity for revitalizing the American economy quickly.

For builders, zones offer RFAs engaging all relevant stakeholders for tailored regulation, speeding groundbreakings. This invites repatriation of whole industries, including the smaller companies that supply larger ones in the zone. One key advantage: By having the anchor tenant negotiate the RFA on behalf of future tenants, that large actor can trigger US Investment Accelerator assistance where small companies alone cannot.

Zones, including Maritime Prosperity Zones (MPZs) under Executive Order 14269, Restoring America’s Maritime Dominance, will bolster national security as well as the resilience of the US economy. Candor negotiates RFAs. We designed the model.

02

Voluntary Pilots

Voluntary Pilots can accelerate EO 14192 by allowing individual projects to proceed without lengthy notice-and-comment rulemaking, exempt as non-binding policy statements under the Administrative Procedure Act (APA). These opt-in experiments test innovative regulatory approaches on a smaller scale, improving processes safely without lowering environmental standards.

Lawful precedent includes EPA’s Project XL from the 1990s, under which 50 experimental pilots were approved; a 1993 DC Circuit case held that voluntary pilots do not require notice and comment. If one administration could approve 50, this one can approve 250, focusing on energy, electricity, data centers, minerals, shipbuilding, and other infrastructure.

Fifty pilots were implemented at EPA, and 20 percent of them eventually led to permanent regulatory changes through notice and comment, demonstrating that temporary, voluntary experiments can lawfully test reforms while paving the way for broader adoption.

This tool allows deregulation to occur project by project, without waiting to refine the process for everyone. It proves better modes of governance quickly. Candor identifies how your projects fit a pilot and brings them to regulators ready to run one.

Permit by Rule

Permit by Rule (PBR) grants automatic approval for projects to move forward if predefined criteria are met, improving enforcement while reducing permitting time.

It focuses regulators on upfront analysis, ongoing evaluation, and quick enforcement. Used by EPA and at least 38 states, PBR streamlines lower-risk activities, enhancing accountability without lowering standards. In Texas, a permit by rule clears in 30 to 45 days; the standard air permit takes four to nine months. That speed drove petrochemical growth.

This tool is the centerpiece of the 5 Tools: The first two enable it, and the final two support it. Regulators shift from analysis paralysis to efficient enforcement. Candor’s principal wrote the white papers behind the FREE Act, the PBR bill now before Congress.

Transparency Technology

Transparency Technology provides real-time monitoring and reporting of emissions, authentications, and other compliance through networked sensors and tamper-evident records, making verification provable and automating oversight. Sensors can be identified by their unique signatures and people by their biometrics, for better trust and accountability.

It also lets an eGov portal issue permits automatically under PBR, verifying that threshold conditions are met, such as the surety bond below. This reduces opacity without lowering standards, and it can shave years off the time to permit.

A “courthouse in the cloud” can verify compliance in real time, cutting review times from years to milliseconds.

Builders already put sensors into so many parts of their operations. It is time for regulation to catch up, lowering reporting costs while increasing accountability and accuracy. Candor designs the monitoring package that makes your RFA or PBR application credible.

05

Surety Bonds

Surety bonds can serve as an alternate form of compliance with NEPA’s statutory environmental analysis requirements, which average 4.5 years. And they can do so while protecting the environment and the taxpayer against the risk of accidents. Shifting the analysis to private-sector underwriters improves quality and speed. Requiring an applicant to post a bond that guarantees compliance would be a step-change improvement in environmental accountability.

Bonds keep skin in the game where it belongs: With the builders assuring they will comply. And they give officials confidence that they have met their statutory obligation to balance environmental effects with the need for human progress. Candor is in talks with surety underwriters eager to connect your project to a bond-backed pilot.
