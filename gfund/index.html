Gfund

Make the willing able.

Candor LLC is preparing the ground to launch Gfund: A vertically integrated development fund for investing in federal zones and Allied Opportunity Zones (AOZs) with US-friendly investment, security, and rule of law.

Rebalancing global trade presents an urgent need for investment in reshoring, nearshoring, and friendshoring: Bringing manufacturing to the US, to neighboring countries, and to our allies around the world.

We invest where there is an unmet need for security, legal, human resources, or physical infrastructure. By bringing these improvements, we can develop capital investments that dramatically increase the underlying value of land and labor in and around a zone. We negotiate a Regulatory Framework Agreement (RFA) with eGov services to protect the property rights of investors, entrepreneurs, and workers, while delivering transformative political wins to sovereign leaders and added value for an anchor tenant.

We are building a team and a pipeline of promising areas to invest.
